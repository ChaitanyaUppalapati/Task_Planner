## What's new in 1.1.0

- **In-app updates** — Task Planner now checks for new versions and installs them for you (Settings → Updates, or tray → Check for updates). Downloads are verified with SHA-256 before installing.
- **Interactive tutorial** — a guided tour of the app runs after install; start it any time with 🎓 Tutorial in the sidebar, the tray menu, Settings, or F1.
- **Weekly targets** — a weekly output goal and weekly task commitments with a weekly score and bonus.
- Fix: Claude breakdown could hang on "thinking" forever.
- Fewer antivirus false positives; installer shows the license.
